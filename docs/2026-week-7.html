Ratatouille Rot: Dungeon Delivers a Stinker in Week 7

The Dungeon Ratatouille coughed up a win to the struggling Team o’ Crims, proving that even a 2-5 record can smell blood.
By Vince Malloy · Beat Writer, The Hetero Herald

The Dungeon Ratatouille proved this week that even when the table is set for success, some chefs just prefer to serve up a disaster. A 4-3 squad, sitting comfortably in a playoff spot at 6th, managed to lose by a mere 6.26 points to Team o’ Crims, a team whose season thus far has been less a "comeback story" and more a "cautionary tale" at 2-5 and 10th in the standings.

This isn’t just a loss; it’s a public service announcement for poor decision-making. To drop 131.18 points and still come up short against a team clinging to the faint hope of not being mathematically eliminated before Thanksgiving, it suggests a profound misunderstanding of the league’s most basic principles.

While Team o’ Crims will celebrate their 137.44 point performance, clawing their way to 2-5, the real story here is the playoff contender that let them do it. The Dungeon Ratatouille now holds a 4-3 record, their grip on a playoff spot weakened, all thanks to a Week 7 misstep that will undoubtedly haunt their seeding discussions down the stretch.

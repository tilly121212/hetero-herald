The King’s Dissapointment Crumbles Under Dungeon Rat OG’s Onslaught

Week 2 delivered a public service announcement: some teams are built to contend, others are merely built to be beaten.
By Vince Malloy · Beat Writer, The Hetero Herald

The Dungeon Rat OG didn’t just win their Week 2 matchup; they carved a monument to managerial competence, delivering a devastating 162.98 points against a team that seemingly forgot what Sunday was for. The King’s Dissapointment mustered a meager 100.16 points, demonstrating a level of performance that can only be described as a complete and utter abdication of competitive spirit, leaving them gasping in the dust of a staggering 62.82-point margin.

Now at a fragile 1-1, The King’s Dissapointment has much to ponder, specifically how a lineup could be so thoroughly outclassed that it didn't just lose, but actively contributed to its own public humiliation, turning a promising start into a Week 2 disaster class. This wasn’t a close contest where a few bounces went the other way; this was a fundamental failure to compete against a 2-0 team clearly operating on a different plane.

The Dungeon Rat OG, now comfortably at 2-0, didn't just beat an opponent; they issued a stern, unequivocal warning to the rest of the league, while simultaneously exposing The King’s Dissapointment as a team whose name might be less a moniker and more a prophecy.

"That wasn’t a loss; that was an audit, and the numbers failed spectacularly."
The King delivered on his name. We merely collected. Two and counting.— The Dungeon Rat OG

Game of the Week

A Crown is Kept, a Glove Slips

The King’s Dissapointment clung to a victory by the slimmest of margins, leaving 1loveglove to lament 11.18 points left on the field or simply, in the ether.
By Vince Malloy · Beat Writer, The Hetero Herald

The Week 6 main event saw The King’s Dissapointment escape with a 124.06 to 112.88 victory over 1loveglove, a margin of just 11.18 points. For a team now sporting a 5-1 record and holding the third seed, a win is a win. But for 1loveglove, now 4-2 and dropping to fifth, this was the kind of soul-crushing defeat that leaves you staring at your lineup and wondering what might have been.

1loveglove delivered a respectable 112.88 points, enough to beat half the league this week. But not enough for The King’s Dissapointment, who, let's be honest, didn't exactly put up a Goliathan total themselves. The difference, the ever-so-slight nudge, points to either a series of minor errors or the cruel hand of fate, and in this league, the former usually wears the latter's cloak.

The result secures The King’s Dissapointment’s position near the top, a solid contender halfway through. For 1loveglove, the loss ensures they don't climb, and instead fall behind other challengers. This playoff picture is far from settled, but a loss by 11.18 points when you’re pushing for a top seed? That's the kind of arithmetic that haunts a manager well past Sunday night.

"Some losses just remind you that points, like good intentions, often go unfulfilled."
Another set of numbers in our favor. Not much to discuss.— The King’s Dissapointment
